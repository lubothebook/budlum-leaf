## PART 0 · HOW TO READ THIS DIRECTIVE

### 0.1 The promise of this document

This directive takes Budlum Leaf from a sentence to a buildable system. It covers the product law, the geometry, the panel, the body, the energy, the radio and the camera module. It covers the glasses, the field mode, the firmware and the network. It covers the security model, the verification plan and the program that builds it. The **how** is written at the depth of a work order, and not of a brochure. A person or an AI can start work on any part of it. This document and the Budlum repository answer the question already. `[DECISION]`

### 0.4 The one-paragraph system

A Budlum Leaf is a light, flexible and emissive surface. It carries a removable energy sheet, a wallet authority and a radio. The interface is rendered inside the device. The correctness, the storage and the heavy compute run on the Budlum network. The session, the data and the identity move between providers, and the device does not change. `[DECISION]`

### 0.7 What is fixed and what is open

**Fixed:** the geometry, the mass law, the panel, the light technology and the module. **Fixed:** the naming, the design language, the family, the market and the production country. **Fixed:** the network position. **The thickness is an envelope: 4.00 mm is the maximum, and the order states the body thickness inside it** (the audit F-09).
**Open, and not blocking**. The catalogue band is parked by the order of Ayaz. The launch plan is parked. The cost items are deferred. The second source names are a partner question. The three sensor suppliers and the exact cell partner are open.

**The rule**. An open item never blocks a work package with a written order and a gate. It blocks a claim. `[DECISION]`

### 0.76 The external audit pass of 2026-10-05, and the four tools

**The audit**. An external audit of revision K arrived with it: `AUDIT/LEAF-AUDIT-AND-HARDENING.md`, fifteen findings, each with a severity and a work order. **Every finding is closed by an edited file and a changed gate**, and the ledger is Part 19, Section 19.5. **Eight corrections matter most**. The energy density became a band. The strain rule became `d / R`, measured about each layer's own axis. The layers decouple and every interface shears. Polycarbonate keeps the whole face, because its worst strain is 0.10 percent. The book model retired and the wrap at R 25.5 mm replaced it, with the gates B-1 to B-10. The water class became one claim. The thickness became an envelope. The second face, the custom shape and the patent screen gained their rules.

### 0.77 The block of 2026-10-12: 4-Quadrant (`+`) 2.00 mm (`68 g`) solid-state body, universal `Qi2.2`, and `90°`-bent-stem B module (`20 g`)

**Five orders of Ayaz opened Revision L.5.0**. *Cancel the bulky external bottom battery module*. *Split the `2.00 mm` (`68 g`) body across `+` into four `17 g` quadrants with a `<15 µm` (`n = 1.585`) seam*. *Use an eyewear-safe biocompatible `LLZO/PEO` all-solid-state battery (`4 x 3.0 Wh = 12.0 Wh`, `<=36.2 C`, `ISO 10993`)*. *Enable `Qi / Qi2.2` wireless charging on the portless screen body and put `USB Type-C` only in the B module*. *Bend only the B stem (`çubuk`) at `90°` (`dik açıyla`) while its round ring lies flat on the screen (`+Z` or `-Z`)*.

**What landed**.

| The change | Where |
|---|---|
| **portless universal `Qi2.2` wireless charging on the screen body**. Central `Ø54 mm` `Qi2.2` (`15-25 W`) and `4 x Ø22 mm` `Qi` coils charge on any pad; zero USB-C on body | Part 20, Section 20.1 and Section 20.2; `HARDWARE/04`, `HARDWARE/14`, `pafta/P10`, `pafta/P16` |
| **only the stem (`çubuk`) of the B module (`20.0 g`) bends `90°` (`dik açıyla`) while the `Ø32/Ø24 mm` ring lies flat on the screen**. Clips onto all 4 edges and flips between Front (`+Z`) and Back (`-Z`) | Part 20, Sections 20.3.1 to 20.3.5; `HARDWARE/05`, `HARDWARE/13`, `HARDWARE/20`, `pafta/P14`, `pafta/P21` |

| **4-Quadrant (`+`) `2.00 mm` (`68 g`) body + `12.0 Wh` `LLZO/PEO` solid-state battery** (`<15 µm` seam, `<=36.2 C` eyewear limit, `EU 2023/1542`) | Part 4, Part 17, `HARDWARE/11`, `HARDWARE/15`, `pafta/P11`, `pafta/P12` |
