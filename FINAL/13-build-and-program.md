## 13.5 The service and the warranty stance

1. The panel, battery sheet and B module are **user replaceable** with a service kit.
2. The weld and the body are a workshop operation, and the workshop network is part of the pilot.
3. Spare parts run for at least five years after the last sale.
4. Every replaced part updates the component record, and the record is the warranty document.

---

## 13.6 The program charter

1. **The program never closes**. A phase ends; the program does not. Every block produces a deliverable, a record and a next block.
2. **The program lead is the AI**. It plans, writes, checks and reports; Ayaz decides the identity of the product and approves a decision.
3. **Every working block leaves the workspace further ahead**. A block without a new artefact, gate result or decision is logged as defective.
4. **A deliverable is a file, a number and a gate**.
5. **The three lines run at once**. The drawing, software and partner lines each produce a weekly output.

| The line | Now | The next block |
|---|---|---|
| the drawing line | the drawing set P01 to P07 at revision 3.1, and this directive | the prototype set P08 to P14, in the order of Part 14 |
| the software line | this directive and the firmware specification | the Leaf Firmware skeleton: the four tasks, the pools, the record format, the bench tests of LF-3 and LF-4 |
| the partner line | the dossier of Part 13.3 | the six sample orders, the pass conditions, the laboratory plan |

### 13.6.1 The rhythm

| The beat | The output |
|---|---|
| every working block | a new artefact or a new result |
| every week | the gate review: what opened, what closed, what is blocked |
| every month | the program review: the plan, the partners, the risk register, the claims |
| every decision of Ayaz | a revision log and the changed numbers |
| every claim | the claim policy check |

### 13.6.2 The consistency checks

Run the number, tag, name, drawing, plan and claim checks of Part 12, Section 12.6 on every revision.

### 13.6.3 The definition of done

A work package is done when the artefact, test, raw gate data, and B.U.D. record exist. The claim policy must pass, and every part must have a named second source. `[DECISION]`

## 13.7 The risk register

| The risk | The severity | The answer |
|---|---|---|
| the panel partner cannot bond a tile without a visible seam | high | a second partner, and the overlap bond of Part 2 |
| the cell at 900 Wh/L does not survive the tile geometry | high | the plan is written at 750 Wh/L too, and the Feather fill exists |
| the slip bond creeps at 60 °C | medium | the 1 000 h creep measurement, and a bonded fallback at a wider radius |
| the magnet costs more than 1.5 dB of antenna gain | medium | the clip of Part 5, Section 5.3.1 |
| the folded path of the module does not hold 17.4 mm inside 3.40 mm | medium | the fallback ladder: a 4 element path in the same fold, and the plate stays 4.00 mm in every case |
| the clear lens is too dim in the sun | medium | the dimmer, and the honest indoor and cloudy claim |
| the link in a market is not the class of the promise | high | the class statement per market, and the near validator siting |
| the far validator case degrades the feel | medium | the two-budget rule of Part 10: the interaction is local |
| a stub surface ships, like the `src/sdk/` precedent | high | wired from the module root and gated, or it does not exist |
| the costs of a large member break the class | medium | the grade law of Part 2, and cost items stay deferred |
| the Frame is declared a recording device by a market | medium | no camera in the base Frame, and the indicator rule |
| the cassette is classified as two batteries in Europe | medium | the legal review, and the article 11 wording |

## 13.8 The 90 days, and the 12 months

### The 90 days

1. Send the first letters to the panel, battery, clear-panel, optical, Turkish manufacturing, and laboratory partners.
2. Order the coupon set: polycarbonate cover, hard coat, slip adhesive, polymer-gel battery laminate, spine, and clear panel.
3. The anchor verification bench on two candidate secure elements: gate SW-2 starts.
4. The Leaf Firmware specification accepted, and the skeleton started (Part 8, Section 8.8, steps 1 to 4).
5. The order books of the ARGE plan written inside the Budlum tree rules.

### The 12 months

1. Build a working Leaf 160 with the continuous battery sheet, upright B module, and side Port Rail.
2. The slip bond coupon through 100 000 cycles at the class radius.
3. A far tile surface with the seam below the visibility threshold at 2.5 m.
4. The first session handoff between two operators, clean and unplanned.
5. The clear lens with a measured see-through number at 10 000 cd/m², and the Frame decision.
