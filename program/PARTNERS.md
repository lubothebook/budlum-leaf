# BUDLUM LEAF · PROGRAM · THE CANDIDATE PARTNERS

**Revision L.6.0 · 2026-10-12 · the Program Office**. This file names two or more candidate partners per technology. It gives the reason for each. It answers gap G-07 as a plan. A partner enters the program only with a signed NDA, a measured sample and a second source. `[DECISION]` `FINAL/13`, Section 13.3.

**The reading of the tags**. A `[PUBLICATION]` row names a public fact of the partner. A `[GENERAL]` row states a known industry role. A `[HYPOTHESIS]` row is the Program Office judgement of fit. No partner has seen the Leaf yet.

## 1 · The ask of every partner

1. A sample against a named gate of `FINAL/21`, Section 21.8.
2. A quotation at `100 000`, `1 000 000` and `5 000 000` units per year.
3. A second source plan, or a licence that lets a second source build.
4. The L.6.0 target as written. A partner that asks for a lower target joins as a second source only. `[DECISION]`

## 2 · The `508 ppi` micro-LED tile

The ask: `200 x 200` pixels on a `10 x 10 mm` glass carrier, with `5 µm` chips. The edge pixel sits `20 µm` from a cut of `10 µm` or less. The tile runs at `240 Hz` with TGV drive. Gate DSP-19, gap G-40.

| The candidate | The reason | The tag |
|---|---|---|
| **AUO** with **PlayNitride** | AUO ships the micro-LED face of a Garmin watch at about `326 ppi` in 2025. PlayNitride supplies chips and transfer to AUO | `[PUBLICATION]` Garmin and AUO, 2025, cited in `FINAL/21`, Section 21.2.3 |
| **Samsung Display** with Samsung Electronics | Samsung ships The Wall, a modular micro-LED display, since 2018. Tiling and seam calibration are in-house skills | `[PUBLICATION]` Samsung, The Wall, CES 2018 |
| **Tianma** | large LTPS and glass lines in China, and a micro-LED programme on glass | `[GENERAL]` |
| **Jade Bird Display (JBD)** | micro-LED microdisplays at a few µm pitch on silicon. The chip and the fine-pitch epitaxy fit the `5 µm` emitter | `[GENERAL]` JBD Hummingbird microdisplays |
| X Display Company (XDC) | micro-transfer printing licence for chips and for the `28 nm` uIC | `[GENERAL]` |
| Kulicke & Soffa, Coherent | laser mass transfer and laser repair tools | `[GENERAL]` |

**The plan**. Two tile programmes run in parallel to milestone M6: AUO with PlayNitride, and Samsung Display. JBD is the chip second source. `[DECISION]` `PLAN.md`, Section 4.2.

## 3 · The hermetic micro cell

The ask: `9.25 x 9.25 x 0.90 mm`, `30 µm` welded can, alumina and platinum feedthrough, `600 Wh/L` committed, `750 Wh/L` target with Si-C. Gates ENR-22 and ENR-23, gap G-41.

| The candidate | The reason | The tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Enovix** | the AI-1 silicon-anode phone cell measures `935 Wh/L`. Its 3D cell and laser-cut electrodes match the `±10 µm` overhang need | `[PUBLICATION]` Enovix AI-1, Polaris Battery Labs, 13 Jan 2026 |
| **Amprius** | silicon nanowire and SiCore cells. Amprius states up to `500 Wh/kg` and `1 300 Wh/L` | `[PUBLICATION]` Amprius product releases |
| **TDK** with ATL | TDK announced an oxide solid-state material for small cells near `1 000 Wh/L` in 2024. ATL is a leading phone cell maker | `[PUBLICATION]` TDK press release, June 2024 |
| **Murata** | small solid-state and Li-ion cells for wearables, and a large ceramic process base | `[GENERAL]` |
| **Ilika** | Stereax micro solid-state cells for medical implants. Hermetic micro packaging is its core skill | `[PUBLICATION]` Ilika Stereax product line |
| Kyocera, Morgan Advanced Materials | co-fired alumina feedthroughs with platinum vias, an implant class part | `[GENERAL]` |

**The plan**. Enovix or Amprius brings the Si-C anode. A can and feedthrough partner from the implant world builds the envelope. Ilika and TDK run the solid-state option as a second path. `[HYPOTHESIS]` gate ENR-22.

**The custom TCM**. The tile protection IC starts from a shipping protection IC with FETs. `[GENERAL]` Candidates: Texas Instruments, ABLIC, Renesas.
