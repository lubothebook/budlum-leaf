# BUDLUM LEAF · BATTERY-FREE PANEL · FOUNDER QUESTIONS

**Revision L.8 · 2026-10-20**. These questions do not block the engineering work. The Program Lead uses the stated default until a new order changes it. `[DECISION]`

| No. | Question for the founder | Engineering default | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-BP-1 | Does "about 60 V input" mean a rated direct-current input, a USB-C negotiation target, or an insulation test limit? | Rate the Core direct-current input for up to `60 V`. Negotiate only a certified standard mode on USB-C. Do not claim USB-C offers `60 V` without a source. | **NEEDS TEST** |
| Q-BP-2 | Does "panels of any size" include sustained full-brightness wall panels powered only by one portable Core? | Keep electrical compatibility across sizes. Restrict battery operation by the measured Core power limit. Use a qualified Core or mains feed for a larger panel. | **NEEDS TEST** |
| Q-BP-3 | Does a prescription lens need direct optical emission in the lens, or can a PC waveguide carry light from a frame-mounted microdisplay? | Keep prescription correction in the lens. Test both optical paths, with the Core on the frame and no lens cell. | **NEEDS TEST** |
| Q-BP-4 | Is the target "attach at any point" literal, or is an edge/front/rear zone on each panel sufficient? | Use qualified, sealed zones. A continuous all-area link adds traces and heat to the bending panel. | **NEEDS TEST** |
| Q-BP-5 | What is the permitted patent risk if no verified patent-free path exists for a stack or connector? | Do not claim freedom to operate. Test a design-around and require counsel clearance before manufacture. | **NEEDS TEST** |
