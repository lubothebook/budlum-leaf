## 2 · The Field mode, explained and sold

**What it is**. A Leaf 160 or a Leaf 160² has two faces. A small spacer of lens and a hinge holds the device at the eyes. Each face serves one eye, forming a **wide, bright stereo window** of 85 to 100 degrees that surrounds the user.

**Why it is not a metaverse**. Nothing is replaced. The world is not photographed, not rebuilt and not hidden. The user holds a real surface in front of the eyes, and one gesture takes it away. There is no account in a second world, no avatar, no walled room. The isolation rule is written in Volume III, Section 6: one action enters, one action leaves, and a program can never place the user inside.

**What it is for, in the words a person uses**.

| The moment | What the Field mode gives |
|---|---|
| a film on a plane or in a bed | a 100 degree screen that no seat, no wall and no small room allows |
| a stereo photograph of a place the user has been | the depth that a flat surface cannot show, from the camera of the user |
| a map that the user walks through | a wide field with the route in front and the world one gesture away |
| a game | the widest surface of the family at the lowest cost, because the device is the same device |
| a lesson, a diagram, a model | a life size object in the room, seen from any side |
| a quiet moment | a large calm image, with the outside world one gesture away |

**What it costs**. A spacer of 28 to 45 g. No new panel, no new board, no second product. It is an accessory, and the cheapest immersive screen that the program can build.

**Its honest limits, printed with the claim**.

1. The density is about 27 ppd per eye across 85 degrees on a Leaf 160. It is a cinema and a scene mode, not a fine text mode.
2. With both faces near the eyes, the device sits in the hand or a light mount, never in walking mode.
3. Motion-to-photon latency sums the session, link and panel, with a target below 45 ms. Above that, the mode states a reduction instead of pretending.

**The name of the product moment**. *Leaf Field: the field of view of a room, in a device that fits a pocket, with the world one gesture away.*

**The positioning rule**. The Field mode is sold as **the widest surface of the family**, never as a world. It answers what only this device can do: surround the eyes with light from its own two faces.

## 3 · The parametric size, written as law

1. A catalogue class names a reference size, and the class exists for the catalogue, the tooling and the drawings.
2. **The order fixes the size**. A user can order any size inside the tooling band, and the member carries that number.
3. Tooling spans **140x70 to 180x90 mm** carried and **1200x700 to 2000x1200 mm** large. `[HYPOTHESIS]` the tooling band of the panel partner fixes the final numbers, and gate DSP-10 checks it.
4. A member outside the band is a project, not a catalogue item, and it is quoted as a project.
5. The geometry manifest of Volume I, Section 9 fixes the size across sessions, drawings and records.
