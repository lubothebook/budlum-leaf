# BUDLUM LEAF · MASTER DIRECTIVE · VOLUME I · PRODUCT, FORMATS AND DISPLAY

**Version:** 4.1 (revision A, the answers of Ayaz of 2026-10-05) · part of `MASTER/00-INDEX.md`
**Scope**. What a Budlum Leaf is, the format family, the panel and tile law, the sharpness law, the light technology and the large-surface path. Every number fits the geometry Ayaz fixed: **the carried form is 80 mm by 160 mm**, and the panel runs to **144 Hz**.

---

## 1 · The product law

1. A Budlum Leaf is a **surface that shows, a wallet that authorizes and a radio that connects**. `[DECISION]`
2. The body holds no heavy processor, operating system, camera, speaker or cooling part. `[DECISION]`
3. The words *phone*, *tablet*, *television*, *monitor*, *laptop*, *computer*, *VR headset* and *smart glasses* are not product names in this program. A user says: **"I use a Budlum Leaf"**. Abroad, 80 by 160 mm is the size class of a hand-held surface. In this program it is a **Leaf 160**, and the naming law is part of the product. `[DECISION]`
4. **The name is the product name plus the size that the order fixes**. A catalogue class names a reference size: Leaf 80, Leaf 160, Leaf 160², Leaf 480, Leaf 1400 and Leaf 2800. **The family is parametric**: the user chooses the size, and the member carries that number. The worn member is the Leaf Frame, the only member named with a word (Ayaz, questions 12 and 14). `[DECISION]`
5. **The size of a carried member is an order parameter, and the tooling covers a band, not a single size**. The reference carried size is 80 by 160 mm, and Volume V states the first tooling band.
6. **No English word and no Turkish word for a device class enters a format name**. A file name, a drawing, a claim and a code identifier follow the same rule.

### 1.1 The form language, fixed by Ayaz

| The property | The value | The reason |
|---|---|---|
| The corner radius | **R = 6.35 mm** on the 4 vertical corners of the carried member (`32` in `0.1 mm` design units) | keeps the screen rectangular with no giant oval corners |
| The silhouette | **an `80.0 x 160.0 x 4.00 mm` rectangular prism with all 12 edges softened (`r = 0.80 mm`)** | no sharp 90 degree angle meets the hand on any edge or corner |
| The transitions | every horizontal and vertical edge blends smoothly (`r = 0.80 mm` fillet, `R = 6.35 mm` corner) | |
| The finish | **mat polymer with an anti-fingerprint texture**, and the hardness comes from the coat, not from a gloss | the answer of Ayaz, question 13 |
| The colours | a neutral set: a deep black, a warm white, a sand, and one saturated accent per season; no logo on the face | |
| The fasteners | none visible: the weld closes the body, and the battery sheet and upright B module are the service paths | |
| The clear variant | the same outline in a smoky or a clear polymer, with the clear panel of Volume III | |

**The rule**. The form language belongs to the profile and holds at every size, with the same softened edges and corners. `[DECISION]`

## 2 · The format family

The family is built from **two panels** and **one far tile**. Section 3 defines them. Every format is a count of those parts, one bus, one energy cassette and one radio hub.

| Format | The face | Geometry | Diagonal | Panel set | Pixels | Density | Distance | ppd | Display power | Display mass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Leaf 80** | 80 by 80 mm | two faces, one cassette between | 4.4 in | 2 × P80 | 2.56 Mp per face | 508 ppi | 25 cm | 87 | 0.6 to 1.1 W | 6 g |
| **Leaf 160** | 160 by 80 mm | two faces, one cassette between | 7.04 in | 2 × P160 | 5.12 Mp per face | 508 ppi | 30 cm | 104 | 0.9 to 1.8 W | 12 g |
| **Leaf 160²** | 160 by 160 mm | two faces, four panels, two cassettes, the wrap zone in the middle | 8.9 in | 4 × P160 | 10.2 Mp per face | 508 ppi | 40 cm | 130 | 2 to 4 W | 23 g |
| **Leaf 480** | 480 by 240 mm | one face, powered rail | 21.1 in | 72 × T40 B | 4.15 Mp | 152 ppi | 60 cm | 63 | 6 to 12 W | 0.10 kg |
| **Leaf 1400** | 1 400 by 800 mm | one face, powered rail, buffer cassette | 63 in | 700 × T40 C | 6.45 Mp | 61 ppi | 250 cm | 105 | 45 to 160 W | 0.9 kg |
| **Leaf 2800** | 2 800 by 1 600 mm | one face or a tiled wall | 127 in | 2 800 × T40 C | 25.8 Mp | 61 ppi | 400 cm | 168 | 240 to 600 W | 3.6 kg |

**Notes**.

1. `ppd` is pixels per degree of view; Section 4 proves the arithmetic.
2. The two faces of a carried format are driven as **one logical surface**. In the closed state one face is live. In the open state, where the geometry allows it, both faces form one canvas across the fold. **Both faces carry the same panel**, so turning the surface over keeps the same sharpness (Ayaz, question 10). `[DECISION]`
3. Carried formats and Desk run at **144 Hz** down to 1 Hz. Room and Wall run at 120 Hz and 60 Hz to avoid wasted energy on a 63-inch panel. `[DECISION]`
4. Mass is the display only. Volume II adds the cassette, the spine, the seals, the hub and the mount.

## 3 · The panel and tile law

### 3.1 The two panels

| Panel | Geometry | Pixels | Density | Pitch | Thickness | Mass | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P80** | 80 by 80 mm | 1 600 × 1 600 = 2.56 Mp | 508 ppi | 50 µm | 0.35 mm | 2.6 g | Pocket, the Frame lens, a control surface |
| **P160** | 160 by 80 mm | 3 200 × 1 600 = 5.12 Mp | 508 ppi | 50 µm | 0.35 mm | 4.6 g | Hand, Book |

A carried Leaf therefore has **no seam at all**: one panel per face. This is the central change of revision A, and it removes the hardest process problem of the near field. `[DECISION]`

### 3.2 The far tile

| Grade | Geometry | Pixels | Density | Pitch | The use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T40 B** | 40 by 40 mm | 240 × 240 = 0.058 Mp | 152 ppi | 166 µm | Desk |
| **T40 C** | 40 by 40 mm | 96 × 96 = 0.0092 Mp | 61 ppi | 416 µm | Room, Wall |

One driver per tile, one bus, one bonding process. A Grade C tile costs a fraction of Grade A because mask, driver lines and addressing power fall with pitch.

### 3.3 The seam law

A tile carries a non-emitting border for the seal and the bond. A seam is invisible when the bond line is **narrower than one pixel at the viewing distance**.

| Surface | One pixel at the distance | A 0.12 mm bond line in pixels | The verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leaf 160, 30 cm | 50 µm | 2.4 px | visible: therefore a single panel per face |
| Leaf 160², 40 cm | 50 µm | 2.4 px | visible: therefore the seam sits inside the wrap zone, where the tiles already part by 1.20 mm |
| Leaf 480, 60 cm | 166 µm | 0.72 px | invisible |
| Leaf 1400, 2.5 m | 416 µm | 0.29 px | invisible |
| Leaf 2800, 4 m | 416 µm | 0.29 px | invisible |

**The rule**. Tiling is for the far formats. The near formats use one panel per face, and the one seam of a Leaf 160² sits inside the wrap zone. `[DECISION]`

### 3.4 The bus and the hub

| Part | Function | Value |
|---|---|---|
| The spine | structure, bus, heat path, antenna ground, magnetic anchor | Volume II, Section 3 |
| The hub H1 | the decoder, the tile or panel scheduler, the local scene renderer, the frame reprojection unit | 2.5 g, 0.7 to 1.4 W, one per carried Leaf |
| The hub H3 | the same, for 128 tiles | 40 g, 4 to 9 W, one per Room or Wall |

`[DECISION]` The hub is fixed function. It has no user code and no operating system. Running unsigned code on a Leaf is an architecture defect that shifts security back to the device.

## 4 · The sharpness law

### 4.1 The arithmetic

```
mm per degree = d × 0.01746     (d in mm, small angle)
ppi           = 25.4 / pitch in mm
ppd           = (ppi / 25.4) × mm per degree
```

### 4.2 The table

| Format | Distance | mm per degree | ppd | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leaf 80 | 250 mm | 4.36 | 87 | premium |
| Leaf 160 | 300 mm | 5.24 | 104 | premium |
| Leaf 160 | 400 mm | 6.98 | 140 | beyond the eye |
| Leaf 160² | 400 mm | 6.98 | 140 | beyond the eye |
| Leaf 480 | 600 mm | 10.47 | 63 | good |
| Leaf 480 | 800 mm | 13.97 | 84 | premium |
| Leaf 1400 | 2 500 mm | 43.65 | 105 | premium |
| Leaf 1400 | 3 500 mm | 61.11 | 147 | beyond the eye |
| Leaf 2800 | 4 000 mm | 69.84 | 168 | beyond the eye |

**The rule**. Every format delivers at least 60 ppd at its intended distance, and the surface states the number. `[DECISION]`

### 4.3 The reading that Ayaz chose: the hybrid rule

Ayaz answered question 3 with the hybrid rule. It is written as law:

1. **The family standard is the angular reading**. The surface is sharp for the eye at the intended distance. A Leaf 1400 at 2.5 m delivers 105 ppd, sharper than 104 ppd at 30 cm.
2. **A dense surface is a specialist order**. A room or wall watched from 1 m uses a denser grade, with pixel count, heat and power stated. The architecture is the same; only the grade changes.
3. **The proof of the cost of the linear reading**, so that the refusal is documented:

| Surface | Density | Pixels | Against the 4K class | Addressing power | Panel class |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leaf 1400 63 in, Grade C | 61 ppi | 6.45 Mp | 0.8× | 4 W | 1× |
| Leaf 1400 63 in, 4K class | 68 ppi | 8.3 Mp | 1.0× | 6 W | 1.2× |
| Leaf 1400 63 in, Grade B | 152 ppi | 42 Mp | 5.1× | 30 W | 6× |
| Leaf 1400 63 in, 508 ppi | 508 ppi | 466 Mp | 56× | 330 W | 70× |

`[HYPOTHESIS]` for the addressing power curve. Gate DSP-2 measures it and replaces the estimate. On a large surface, linear density costs 56 to 70 times the panel for a difference the eye cannot see.

### 4.4 The four numbers that define quality, beside the density

1. **Colour volume**. 92 percent of Rec.2020 on carried formats, 85 percent on Desk, 80 percent on Room and Wall. `[HYPOTHESIS]`
2. **Brightness**. 1 500 nits on a 10 percent window and 800 nits full field on carried formats (`700 nits` on Room).
3. **Refresh**. 144 Hz on carried formats and Desk (`1 Hz` minimum), and 120 Hz on Room and Wall. `[DECISION]`
4. **Uniformity**. Far tiles differ by under 2 percent, and seam lines stay below the visibility threshold of Section 3.3. Gate DSP-3.

## 7 · The power model of a surface

```
P_display = area × luminance × APL / (emitter efficacy × optical efficiency)
P_address = lines × refresh × capacitance × the panel factor     (measured in gate DSP-2)
P_hub     = the decoder, the local scene renderer and the reprojection unit
P_radio   = the link class of the session (Volume IV)
```

## 8 · The large-surface path

### 8.1 The structure of a Leaf 1400

| Part | Mass | The note |
|---|---|---|
| 700 Grade C tiles, bonded in columns of 20 | 0.9 kg | 1.3 g per tile, the panel and the seal |
| 35 spine segments | 1.4 kg | the perforated steel and copper laminate |
| One continuous 0.15 mm polymer cover over the face | 0.9 kg | one sheet, no visible seam from the front |
| The rail, the mount and the cassette bay | 0.9 kg | the only rigid part |
| The hub H3, the radio, the connectors | 0.15 kg | |
| **Total** | **4.25 kg** | one person hangs it on a magnetic registration plate |

`[COMPANY]` In 2026, a 65-inch consumer OLED weighs 17 to 24 kg with its stand because it uses glass and steel. The Leaf removes the glass and the frame, and that is the whole saving.

### 8.2 The heat, the mount and the service

1. A 73 W surface with 20 percent loss is 15 W over 11 200 cm², which is 0.0013 W/cm². Natural convection is enough, and no tile can be covered by an insulating layer. `[GENERAL]`
2. The mount is a magnetic registration plate with two safety hooks. One person, no tool, under two minutes.
3. A tile is replaced from the front with a suction tool and a ribbon cable, in under 15 minutes. Gate SRV-2.
4. A Leaf 1400 travels as two columns of tiles in a flat box of 150 cm, at 4 kg.

### 8.3 The feed

Ayaz chose the **rail**: one power and data path runs through the surface, and the buffer cassette keeps the session alive through a power cut. `[DECISION]` The rail carries 48 V and the serial pixel link, and each column draws fused power from the rail.

## 9 · The format change law

1. The identity, the protocol, the permission set and the data location never change with the format. `[DECISION]`
2. **The edge coupler** joins two Leaf surfaces within 0.1 mm and carries power, ground and a two-lane data link. It is the same part on every format.
3. **The dock tree**. A Leaf 160 docks to a Leaf 480 rail and becomes a control surface. A Leaf 160² docks to a Leaf 1400 rail and becomes a second surface or a control surface.
4. **The session moves, not the device**. A session is bound to the user. A surface change has a grace period, and the old surface holds the last frame until acknowledged. Volume IV, Section 3.
5. **The geometry manifest** states the outline, panel map, grade, seam map, keep-out band, bend band, refresh range and calibration. It is signed and stored in B.U.D. `[TREE]` A manifest without a bend band refuses a bend claim.
6. **The count rule**. Two Leaf 80s joined at the edge behave as one Leaf 160: one session, two panels, one bus. A user buys a panel, not a product. `[DECISION]`
