## 1 · The thickness answer, first

| The question | The answer |
|---|---|
| Does the Field mode change the device thickness? | **No**. The 4-Quadrant (`+`) screen body stays `2.00 mm` (`68 g`), the `90°`-bent-stem B module ring stays `4.00 mm`, and the mass law does not change. Field mode is a 28 to 45 g lens spacer that holds the two faces at the eyes (`<=36.2 °C` ocular thermal limit, `LLZO/PEO` solid-state). Nothing in the device changes |
| Does it change the boot, the session or the wallet? | No. It is a surface mode, declared in the geometry manifest, exactly like a change of format |
| Does it change the power? | Yes, and only upward: the two faces run together at a video class, which is the accounting of Volume II, Section 5 |

## 2 · Why a user who already has a carried surface wants it

The eye has one measure: the angle a surface occupies. Two numbers settle the question.

```
the angle of a surface   = 2 × arctan( half width / distance )
```

| What the user looks at | The width | The distance | The angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| a Leaf 160, held in the hand | 160 mm | 300 mm | **30°** |
| a Leaf 160 in the Field mode | 160 mm per eye at the eye | 20 mm | **85 to 100° of the field, the face overfills the eye** |
| a Leaf 1400, a room surface | 1 400 mm | 2 500 mm | **31°** |
| a Leaf 2800, a wall surface | 2 800 mm | 4 000 mm | **39°** |
| a 100 inch screen | 2 214 mm | 2 500 mm | **48°** |
| the same angle as the Field mode, on a wall | 7 150 mm | 3 000 mm | **100°, which is a 320 inch image at three metres** |

**The sentence that sells it**. *The room surface is 31 degrees and stays at home. The carried surface is 30 degrees and fits a pocket. The Field mode is 100 degrees and fits the same pocket.*

**The four reasons a person uses it**.

1. **The size that no room allows**. In a hotel room, seat or bed, Field mode gives a 320-inch image at 3 m without a wall.
2. **A private and a silent screen**. One person watches, nobody else hears or sees. In a shared room that is the difference between an option and no option.
3. **The depth that a flat surface cannot show**. Field mode uses both faces as two eyes for stereo photos, stereo video and 3D models.
4. **The same device, the same session, the same wallet**. Nothing is re-bought or re-logged, and a desk film moves directly into Field mode without copying.

**What it is not**. It is not a second world, not an avatar, not a room that replaces the room. The user holds two faces, and one gesture brings the world back. There is no account to open and no wall to build.

**The honest limits, printed with the sale**. At 27 ppd per eye on a Leaf 160, it is a cinema and scene mode, not fine text. The device is in the hand or in a light mount. The latency target is below 45 ms of motion to photon. `[DECISION]`

## 3 · Why the Field mode is the cheapest immersive screen in the program

| The comparison | The parts that must be bought | The mass | The angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Field mode | one spacer: two lenses, a frame, an adjuster | 28 to 45 g | 85 to 100° |
| A separate immersive product | two panels, two lenses, a board, a battery, a strap, a radio | 400 to 700 g | 90 to 110° |
| A large surface | the surface, the rail, the mount, the wall | 4.25 kg and a wall | 31 to 39° |

**The rule**. The Field mode is sold as **the widest surface of the family**, and its price of entry is an accessory, not a product. `[DECISION]`

## 4 · The Field mode and the Clear Leaf family

A clear face in the Field mode is refused by design. In Field mode the user chose the film, and a transparent face would show the room behind it. The manifest refuses the combination: a clear face is the **world plus a note**, while Field mode is the **note alone**. Two different intentions, two different members. `[DECISION]`
