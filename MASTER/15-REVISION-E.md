## 2 · The two doctrines that this revision added

### 2.1 The latency doctrine, in one line

**The interaction budget is local and hard, while the processing budget is remote and soft. Putting a photograph on the hard budget was the error**. `[DECISION]`

### 2.2 The heat doctrine, in one line

**Heavy compute never enters the device. The module is the hot spot, coupled to the leaf radiator and buffered for bursts**. `[DECISION]`
