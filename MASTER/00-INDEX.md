## 2 · The three laws of the program

1. **The naming law**. Formats use **Budlum Leaf** plus a size (**Leaf 80** through **Leaf 2800**, plus **Leaf Frame**). Words like *phone*, *tablet*, *television*, *monitor*, *laptop*, *computer*, *VR headset* and *smart glasses* never appear in product claims or identifiers. A user says: "I use a Budlum Leaf."
2. **The angular sharpness law**. Every format delivers at least 60 pixels per degree at its intended distance, and states the number. Ayaz chose the hybrid reading: the standard is the eye reading, and a dense surface is a specialist order.
3. **The mass theorem**. Every gram that is not display, not energy, not radio or not anchor is a design error. The proof is the `68 g` (`2.00 mm`) 4-Quadrant (`+`) solid-state body and `88 g` complete assembly of Volume II.

## 3 · How to read a number

`[DECISION]` fixed by Ayaz or by this directive. `[PUBLICATION]` measured or peer reviewed. `[COMPANY]` a supplier statement. `[TREE]` an artefact read from the Budlum repository. `[HYPOTHESIS]` a design target that a gate must turn into a measurement. `[GENERAL]` physics. A number without a tag is a defect. A public claim without a gate is a defect.

### 7.1 The three consequences that Ayaz asked for by name

1. **The mass fell**. In Revision L.5.0, the Leaf 160 `2.00 mm` 4-Quadrant (`+`) body weighs **68 g** (`4 x 17 g`). With the `20 g` `90°`-bent-stem B module, it weighs **88 g**.
2. **The five hour figure was a video session**. Four `LLZO/PEO` solid-state quadrants pack **`12.0 Wh` (`3 200 mAh`)** at `750 Wh/L` (`+ 0.45 Wh` B bridge cell). With Zero CPU, delta sessions run **>14.1 h**. Volume II, Section 5.
3. **Distance independence is now a mechanism, not a hope**. The device renders the interface locally and reprojects the last frame at 144 Hz. An **edge agent** in the dock holds the near session while the network verifies far compute. Volume IV-B, Section 4.
