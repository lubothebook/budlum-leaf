## 3 · The new consequences in the program

1. **The near services entered the validator list of the work packages**. WP-32 of Volume V adds validator near-serving (cache, document model, video buffer and relay) with its own stake. No new role, no new system, no new chain.
2. **The Frame is on the critical path beside the carried product**. The clear panel and lens integration join the PG-0 coupon set because the Frame lens ships first.
3. **The panel buy is simpler**. One panel type serves both carried faces (P80, P160), plus one clear variant and one two-grade far tile. Five panel articles for the whole family.
4. **The wired path is a compliance asset**. USB-C on the detachable rail satisfies the common charger rule and provides the service port.
