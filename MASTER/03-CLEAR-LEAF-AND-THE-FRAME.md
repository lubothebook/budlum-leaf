## 1 · The requirement law, restated

1. **The user sees the world with the user's own eyes**. A Budlum Leaf adds light. It does not replace the world with a camera image. `[DECISION]`
2. A video pass-through view is refused as a base mode. A camera copy adds delay and limits resolution, colour and dynamic range. The world itself has none of them.
3. **The see-through layer of a Leaf never blocks the world**. The transmission of the clear part is at least 45 percent. `[DECISION]`

---

## 2 · The Clear Leaf panel

A Clear Leaf panel is a **transparent emissive panel** on a clear substrate. Pixels occupy part of the area, and world light passes through the gaps.

| Property | Value | The note |
|---|---|---|
| Transmission of the clear area | 60 to 80 percent | `[COMPANY]` |
| Transmission of the whole panel, pixels included | 45 to 70 percent | the number that decides the product |
| Brightness towards the eye | 300 to 800 nits | `[HYPOTHESIS]` gate CLR-1 |
| Pixel aperture | 30 to 50 percent of the area | the wiring and the thin film transistors are transparent |
| Substrate | a polymer film, or a thin glass | a polymer is required where the part bends |
| Density | the same 508 ppi as the P80 and the P160 | one process family with the family panels |
| Thickness | 0.35 mm | the same as the family panels |
| Mass of a P80 clear panel | 3.0 g | the transparent wiring is slightly heavier than copper |
| Lifetime | 10 000 to 20 000 h | a lower duty cycle than a surface panel |

`[PUBLICATION]` for the material behaviour of transparent emissive displays, `[COMPANY]` for the current product statements, `[HYPOTHESIS]` for the Leaf values.

## 3 · The four uses of one capability

### 3.1 The Frame: a Clear Leaf as the lens

The Frame is not a projector with a combiner. **The Frame is a Clear Leaf panel bonded to a prescription lens**. `[DECISION]`

| Property | Value | Against the waveguide path |
|---|---|---|
| The optical path | the world light passes through the lens; the panel emits towards the eye | the same principle, without a projector and without a waveguide |
| Field of view | the whole lens: 70 to 90 degrees, limited by the panel area and the eye relief | a waveguide gives 30 to 50 degrees |
| Density at the eye | **corrected in Part 15, H-1:** a P80 clear panel gives about 23 ppd over a 70 degree field. The Frame therefore uses a 4K class microdisplay of about 2 400 ppi, which gives about 60 ppd | the density is the pixels across divided by the field across |
| Mass per eye | 8 to 11 g: the panel, the lens and the dimmer | a projector and a waveguide are heavier and bulkier |
| Brightness | 300 to 800 nits, so indoors and on a cloudy day | a projector with a micro display engine reaches 1 000 to 2 500 nits, so it survives a brighter day |
| The honest limit | **brightness and contrast in direct sun** | brightness is the strength of the waveguide path |

**The order chosen by Ayaz (question 9): the Frame lens is the first clear product**. `[DECISION]` **The decision rule of the volume**. The Frame is built on the Clear Leaf lens. A waveguide-and-projector outdoor specialist is a **second variant**, ordered only if gate CLR-3 shows the clear lens fails outdoors. `[DECISION]`

### 3.2 The Leaf Window: a carried Clear Leaf

A carried Leaf can be built with two clear panels. Held like a pane of glass, it shows the world while adding a mark, word, line or label.

| The feature | The value |
|---|---|
| The world | real light, so zero delay and unlimited resolution |
| The overlay | text, a marker, an arrow, a translation, a measurement, a name |
| The alignment without a camera | the inertial unit and the ranging radio give a coarse alignment, enough for a label that follows the surface of the world |
| The alignment with a camera | the camera module under a perception lease gives a fine alignment, and the module is removable so the clear Leaf works without it |
| The use cases | a menu in a window, notes on a machine, a price beside an object, a translation over a sign, a map arrow on the street |

**This is the strongest answer to the "metaverse" question in the whole program**. In a Clear Leaf the world is not a video. It is the world, and the panel is a note written on the glass. `[DECISION]`

### 3.3 A surface over glass

The same clear panel serves over windows, glass doors, shop fronts, machine windows, car glass or museum cases. The surface adds the information and the world stays behind it. `[DECISION]`

### 3.4 The dimmer: a family part

The electrochromic dimmer drops transmission to 20 to 35 percent on demand for sunlight contrast and calm immersion. It is a family part on every clear panel, not a Frame part. `[DECISION]`

## 4 · The daylight proof

The light at the eye is the sum of the world light and the display light:

```
L_eye = L_world × T + L_display
```

| The scene | The world at the eye through a panel of 60 percent | The display must add | The verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| An office, 300 cd/m² | 180 cd/m² | 180 to 400 cd/m² | easy for the clear panel |
| A cloudy street, 2 000 cd/m² | 1 200 cd/m² | 1 200 to 2 500 cd/m² | at the limit of the clear panel |
| A sunlit street, 10 000 cd/m² | 6 000 cd/m² | 6 000 to 15 000 cd/m² | not reachable without the dimmer |
| A sunlit street with the dimmer at 25 percent | 2 500 cd/m² | 2 500 to 5 000 cd/m² | reachable for a text class overlay |

**The conclusion**. A clear panel without a dimmer is an indoor and a cloudy day part. With the dimmer it carries a text class overlay in the sun. A full-colour image in direct sun requires the waveguide variant, and that limit is written in the record. `[DECISION]`

## 5 · The Frame product

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Class | a Leaf with a hub of class H1, one radio, one secure element, no camera in the base model |
| Mass | 30 to 40 g, with the front mass below 17 g |
| Energy | a 2 to 3 Wh cell in the temple, so 4 to 8 h with the display on |
| Power | 0.4 to 1.2 W with the display on |
| Charge | a pogo or a coil in the case, 5 to 10 W |
| Prescription | the lens carries the prescription, or a clip in lens does |
| Input | a temple touch strip, a gesture strip, the voice pick up, the wallet through the normal path |
| Autonomy | a prompt, a message, a map, a translation, a call and the wallet. A film and a game belong to a surface or to the Field mode. |
| The base model records nothing, because it has no camera | and the sensor state of an optional module is a hardware light that a program cannot paint |

## 6 · The Field mode

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| The device | a Leaf 160 or a Leaf 160², both faces in stereo, one spacer |
| The spacer | two lenses and an interpupillary adjuster, 28 to 45 g |
| Field of view | 85 to 100 degrees diagonal |
| Density | a Leaf 160 gives about 27 ppd per eye across 85 degrees: enough for a scene, low for fine text, and the claim says so |
| The honest statement | the Field mode is an immersive mode with a lower angular density than a small surface. It is offered for a scene, a film and a stereo image, not as a working surface. |
| The isolation rule | one action enters, one action leaves, a program cannot place the user inside, and the world is one gesture away |
