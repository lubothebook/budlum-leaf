# BUDLUM LEAF · MASTER DIRECTIVE · VOLUME III · THE FRAME AND THE FIELD

**Version:** 4.0 · 2026-10-05 · part of `MASTER/00-INDEX.md`
**Scope:** the glasses member of the family, the see-through requirement, the optics with numbers, and the Field mode of a Leaf 160 or Book.

---

## 1 · The requirement law

1. **The user sees the world with the user's own eyes**. The Frame adds light to the world. It does not replace the world with a camera image. `[DECISION]`
2. **A video pass-through path is not the primary path of the Frame**. A camera image of the world is a copy with a delay, a resolution limit and a colour limit. It is refused as the base mode, for a reason that can be measured: latency and resolution.
3. Product sentence and acceptance test: **"I wear a Budlum Leaf and I see everything around me."**
4. The words *metaverse*, *isolated*, *virtual world* and *blocked vision* do not describe this product. A claim with one of those words is a defect.

## 2 · The optical paths

| Path | How the world reaches the eye | The display | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Waveguide combiner with a projector in the temple** | through a thin glass or polymer slab that guides the image to the eye and passes the world light | a micro light emitting diode engine, or a laser engine, at the temple | **the first product** `[DECISION]` |
| **B. A transparent emissive lens** | directly, through a transparent emissive stack | the lens itself emits | the second product, when indoor brightness is enough |
| C. A curved mirror over the eye | through a partly reflective curved combiner | a projector | refused: a bulky eye box, and the world light is halved over the whole field |
| D. A camera and an opaque screen | through a camera copy | an opaque panel | refused as the base mode, offered only as the Field mode of Section 5 |

### 2.2 The daylight proof

A display in a transparent combiner adds light on top of the world. The world of a sunlit street is about 10 000 candelas per square metre. For usable contrast, display luminance must match the world because addition is linear (`L_eye = L_world × T + L_display`).

| Scene | The world at the eye, with a 70 percent combiner | The display needs | The result |
|---|---|---|---|
| An office, 300 cd/m² | 210 cd/m² | 300 to 600 cd/m² | easy |
| A cloudy street, 2 000 cd/m² | 1 400 cd/m² | 1 500 to 3 000 cd/m² | reachable with a micro display engine |
| A sunlit street, 10 000 cd/m² | 7 000 cd/m² | 7 000 to 15 000 cd/m² | not reachable at a wearable power |
| A sunlit street with the dimmer at 25 percent transmission | 2 500 cd/m² | 2 500 to 5 000 cd/m² | reachable |

**The conclusion, and the product answer**. A frame without a dimmer is an indoor and a cloudy day product. An outer electrochromic dimmer drops transmission to 20 to 35 percent on demand while keeping attenuated real-world light visible. The dimmer is a user control with a hardware state indicator, and the default is clear. `[DECISION]`

### 2.3 The transparent emissive path

A transparent emissive lens passes 60 to 80 percent of the world light and emits towards the eye at 300 to 800 nits. It is a 0.2 to 0.4 mm part with no projector and no waveguide, and it is beautiful indoors and weak in the sun. `[HYPOTHESIS]` Gate FRA-5 measures it and makes it the second product if indoor numbers hold.

## 3 · The Frame as a member of the family

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Class | a Leaf with a hub of class H1, one radio, one secure element, no camera by default |
| Mass | 35 to 45 g, with a 20 g target for the front |
| Energy | a 2 to 4 Wh cell in the temple, so 3 to 6 h of display with a delta class session |
| Power with the display on | 0.5 to 1.5 W, of which the engine is 0.3 to 0.9 W |
| Charging | a pogo or a coil in the case, 5 to 10 W |
| Prescription | the front lens carries the prescription, or a clip in lens does; the range and the cost are stated in the record |
| Interface | a temple touch and a temple gesture strip, a voice pick up, and the wallet through the normal path |
| Autonomy of the Frame without a Leaf 160 | a message, a map, a prompt, a call, and the wallet. Everything else goes to a surface. |

**The Frame and the world**. The Frame exists to keep the user in the world. It is therefore built for prompts, navigation, translation, a message, a photograph of what the user sees, and a call. It is not built for a film and not for a game. A film and a game belong to a Leaf 1400 or to the Field mode, where the user chooses the immersion. `[DECISION]`

## 4 · Safety, the eye and the people around the user

1. **Photobiological safety**. The engine is measured against IEC 62471 (and its laser annex), and the record states the risk group. `[GENERAL]`
2. **The luminance at the eye in a dark room** is capped by a hardware driver limit, not an application setting. `[DECISION]`
3. **The camera**. The base Frame has no camera, so the base Frame cannot record. An optional camera works only under a perception lease (RG-5) with a hardware indicator light. `[DECISION]`
4. **The recording indication**. A Frame that records shows a front indicator. A Frame that is asked to record without an indicator refuses. `[DECISION]`
5. **The social rules of the family**. Recording is refused in stated contexts, with a visible indicator and no hidden capture. No private-display claim exceeds physics: a faint combiner image is visible in a dark room. `[DECISION]`
6. **The eye comfort rules**. A 60 Hz minimum, interpupillary adjustment, bounded content depth, and rest prompts protect eye comfort. Gate FRA-7 measures the discomfort in a user set.

## 5 · The Field mode

The Field mode is the immersive mode of a Leaf 160 or a Leaf 160²: two faces, two eyes, one spacer.

| Property | Value | The note |
|---|---|---|
| The device | a Leaf 160 or a Leaf 160², both faces in stereo | no new hardware beyond the spacer |
| The spacer | two lenses and an interpupillary adjuster, 28 to 45 g | the cheapest stereo path of the architecture |
| Field of view | about 85 to 100 degrees diagonal | the two faces are close to the eyes |
| Resolution per eye | 4.5 Mp for a Leaf 160 | 2 280 pixels across 85 degrees is about 27 ppd, which is low for fine text and enough for a scene |
| Latency budget | the session, the link and the panel together below 45 ms of motion to photon | gate FRA-6 measures it |
| The honest statement | the Field mode is an immersive mode with a lower angular density than a small surface. It is offered for a scene, a film and a stereo image, and it is not offered as a working surface. | `[DECISION]` |
| The isolation rule | Field mode is opt-in: one action enters or leaves it, no program forces it, and the outside world is one gesture away. | `[DECISION]` |
