## 7 · The operating rhythm

| Rhythm | The content | The output |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | the gate review: the open gates, the raw data, the blockers, the decisions | a decision note with a number |
| Monthly | the program review: the plan, the partners, the risk register, the claims | the revised plan |
| Per order | one branch per work package in the tree, the tests written with the order, the review, no merge without a gate | a merged change and a record |
| Per service event | the user service kit of Volume II, Section 3.3: the panel, the tool and the guide | a replaced part with a record, and the five year parts rule |
| Per claim | the claim policy check | a claim with a gate, or no claim |

**The rule for the coding agents**. Each agent owns one work package, writes spec and tests together, and wires every surface from the module root. The `src/sdk/` precedent is the standing warning. `[TREE]`

**The tree rules for Leaf work**. The tree is English only, with the Turkish baseline file as the single exception. The licence stays as it is. Constitutional invariants hold: no ungranted reads, no governance override, no admin whitelist, no key custody, no plaintext evidence, and island isolation. A Leaf change that needs one of those to bend is refused with the reason in writing. `[TREE]`

**The definition of done**. A work package is done when the artefact, test, raw gate data, and B.U.D. record exist. The claim policy must pass, and every part must have a named second source. `[DECISION]`

## 8 · The plan in three horizons

### 8.1 The 90 days

1. The six answers of Section 12, in writing.
2. The partner short list: one panel partner, one cell partner, one waveguide partner, one contract manufacturer, one laboratory.
3. Order the coupon set: polycarbonate cover, hard coat, slip adhesive, barrier film, spine, and polymer-gel battery laminate.
4. The anchor verification bench on two candidate secure elements: gate SW-2 starts.
5. The Leaf Firmware specification, LF-1 to LF-4, written to the point where an agent can implement a mock.
6. The node profile and the display transport orders written inside the Budlum tree rules.

### 8.2 The 12 months

1. Build a working Leaf 160 with the continuous battery sheet, upright B module, and side Port Rail.
2. The slip bond coupon passing 100 000 cycles at the class radius.
3. The Grade C tile with the seam below the visibility threshold at 2.5 m.
4. The first session handoff between two operators, clean and unplanned.
5. The Frame optics choice with a see-through number at 10 000 cd/m².

### 8.3 The 36 months

1. The family: Pocket, Hand, Book, Desk, Room, Wall, Frame, Field.
2. The certificate set of two markets.
3. A tile line with a stated yield and a second panel source.
4. The session marketplace with the records, the leases, the anchors and the optical path in daily use.
5. The claim list, every claim backed by a gate and a raw data set.

## 10 · The claim policy

A claim needs a gate, a raw data set and a date. Forbidden terms include *all day*, *unbreakable*, *zero latency*, *phone*, *tablet*, *television*, *no hardware*, *VR ready*, *metaverse*, *blocked from the world*, and ungated superlatives. `[DECISION]`

## 11 · The program risk register

| Risk | Severity | The answer |
|---|---|---|
| The panel partner cannot bond a tile with a visible-free seam | high | a second partner, and the overlap bond of Volume I, Section 3.2 |
| The Grade A cell at 900 Wh/L does not survive the tile geometry | high | the plan is written at 750 Wh/L too, and the Feather fill exists |
| The slip bond creeps at 60 degrees Celsius | medium | the creep measurement at 1 000 h, and a bonded fallback at a wider radius |
| The magnet costs more than 1.5 dB of antenna gain | medium | the clip of Volume II, Section 5.3 |
| The waveguide cannot reach a sunlit street within the power budget | medium | the dimmer, and the honest indoor and cloudy claim |
| The link in a target market is not the class of the promise | high | the class statement per market, and the near validator siting |
| The session handoff loses state | medium | the object set of Volume IV, Section 3 and gate SW-5 |
| A stub surface ships, like the `src/sdk/` precedent | high | the rule: wired from the module root and gated, or it does not exist |
| The costs of a large surface break the product class | medium | the grade law of Volume I, Section 3.1, and cost items are deferred by order of Ayaz |
| The Frame is declared a recording device by a market | medium | no camera in the base Frame, and the indicator rule |
| The cassette is classified as two batteries in Europe | medium | the legal review, question Q9 of the directive |
| The program loses the honesty of its claims under sales pressure | high | the claim policy of Section 10, and one owner for it |

## 12 · The first six questions for Ayaz

The questions are written in the plain language of the architect, not of the engineer. The full list, with the reason and the recommendation, is in the `ask_user` form beside this volume.

| No. | The question | The recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Which cassette fill is the default of the family: the light one, the balanced one or the long one? | the balanced one, with the light one as the honest answer to "make it lighter" |
| Q2 | Which format becomes a real product first: the carried one, the desk one, the room one or the worn one? | the carried one, because the tile, the cassette and the weld are proved there |
| Q3 | On a large surface, what does "the same quality" mean: the same sharpness for the eye, or the same pixels per inch? | the eye reading, with the dense panel as a specialist order |
| Q4 | The glasses: a combiner with a projector in the temple, a transparent emitting lens, both in sequence, or later? | the combiner first, the transparent lens second |
| Q5 | How is a large surface powered: every piece with its own cassette, one powered rail, or the wall? | one powered rail with a buffer cassette, and the wall for the largest |
| Q6 | The first light technology: the flexible organic light emitting stack, the micro light emitting diode, or both in sequence? | the organic stack first for the whole family, the micro light emitting diode for the Frame and the bright outdoor surfaces |

**The second set of six** covers form language, modules, pen and keyboard, launcher, service/warranty, and Turkish format naming.
