# BUDLUM LEAF · MASTER DIRECTIVE · VOLUME XI · THE PARTNER DOSSIER

**Version:** 4.2 (revision D) · part of `MASTER/00-INDEX.md`
**Purpose**. The package that goes to a supplier, laboratory or manufacturer. It states what we bring, what we ask, what we measure and what we will not accept. Cost items are deferred by Ayaz, so commercial pricing is negotiated separately using this technical annex.

## 3 · What we bring to the first meeting

1. **The drawing set** `LEAF/pafta/P01` to `P07` and its revision, printed at A2 size.
2. **This master directive** per partner field: Volumes I–III for panels, II for cells, IX for optics, and II/V for manufacturing.
3. **The gate list** of the partner's field, with the pass condition written before the sample is ordered. A partner that sees the pass condition first cannot argue about it later.
4. **The record format**, so the sample arrives with the data that the gate needs.

---

## 4 · What we will not accept

| The refusal | The reason |
|---|---|
| a panel whose seam is wider than one pixel at the intended distance for a near member | it breaks the sharpness law |
| a cell whose gauge can be rewritten | it breaks the record and the revocation of a part |
| a cassette that cannot be removed without a tool | it breaks the removability rule and the European requirement |
| a module that cannot be replaced by the user | the same |
| an optical stack that needs more than the stated module thickness without a written trade | the thickness law is the product |
| a part with no second source and no record field | a program risk with a name |
| a claim without a gate | the claim policy of Volume V, Section 10 |
