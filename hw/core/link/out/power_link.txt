E3. the leaves as a power path (hw/energy 03 Section 2.1: 18 mohm, 1.5 A per leaf, continuous)
  delta   0.95 W at 4.2 V:  0.23 A over 2 VBUS leaves = 0.11 A each (  8 % of rating), leaf loss    0.9 mW
  video   1.57 W at 4.2 V:  0.37 A over 2 VBUS leaves = 0.19 A each ( 12 % of rating), leaf loss    2.5 mW
  peak    3.75 W at 4.2 V:  0.89 A over 2 VBUS leaves = 0.45 A each ( 30 % of rating), leaf loss   14.3 mW
  the leaves carry the Skin power with margin. The pins that matter are the data lanes.

E4. the conductor count of the tether that carries both
  data: 2 lanes x 2 wires (D0, D1) + BC pair + 1 sideband + guard grounds = 8 lines of 100 ohm class
  power: 2 VBUS + 2 GND (flex or round) at 5 V up to 0.5 m; 2 VHV + 2 GND at 12 to 20 V beyond 1 m with a clip buck
  flex 0.3 m with 12 conductors: mass 1.3 g, 6 mm wide at 0.5 mm pitch; round 28 AWG micro bundle, 2 m: 8 g

E5. radios and the cell. No power rides on a radio. The Skin then holds a cell.
  video at 1.57 W plus a 60 GHz end (0.6 W) for 2 h: 4.8 Wh = 19 g of cell at 250 Wh/kg
  video at 1.57 W plus a Wi-Fi 7 end (1.0 W) for 2 h: 5.7 Wh = 23 g
  that is 81 to 97 % of the 5.9 Wh L.6.0 body battery. A radio end gives the Split no mass gain.

E6. a Skin with no cell and a hot-unplug: the safe screen needs 0.12 W for 60 s = 7.2 J = 2 mWh. One tile cell holds 46 mWh.
