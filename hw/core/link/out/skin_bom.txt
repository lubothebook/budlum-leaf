C7. is everything off the screen possible? No. A floor remains.
  The floor: the emitters, 1.28 M micro-ICs, 128 hubs with a DSC decoder, touch and angle analog fronts, row drivers,
  the mesh ports, a seat PHY with a controller, a safe-screen ROM, a thermal trip, a part identity, a bridge cell.
  Reasons: a pixel needs its own driver (line count). A touch grid is analog. A hinge angle is a local analog value.
  The mesh copper limit forces a decoder (A5). A link loss needs a screen that says so, and a thermal limit needs a
  trip that does not wait for the Core.
  Floor silicon: hubs 3.3 cm2, seat controllers 0.4 cm2, identity 0.06 cm2. Floor mass 17 g, thickness 1.00 mm.
