THE RULING FOR THE STUDY [HYPOTHESIS]
  1. The pads win. The Core regulates VBUS and the Skin has no stage. Interface heat at the video load is 3 mW new and 13 mW worn.
  2. The Core must sense the VBUS leaves through the back channel BC and hold 3.90 V at the tiles. The worn drop is 30 mV at the video load.
  3. The U_Q stage costs the Skin 40 mW more than the direct feed at the video load. It buys a lower current for a long cable.
  4. A 2 m flex tether at 12 V adds 13 mW to the cable over the pads and nothing on the Skin beyond the stage.
  5. Qi-class power puts 104 mW on the Skin, 2.0 times the 12 V pads, and gives no pixels. The pixel radio adds 0.68 W to the Skin.
  6. The hybrid cable shares a connector with the pixels. It is the best product shape and it costs the Skin the PHY: +0.19 W.
  7. Every option fails the 1.73 W ceiling at the outdoor video load of 4.79 W. The load, not the interface, causes that.
