THE TIER VERDICT FOR A SKIN [HYPOTHESIS]
  1. Full: never. The Skin holds no energy of its own. No state lives on it, so a power gap loses no chain state.
  2. Buffer: not needed for continuity. A Skin without a Core shows nothing, because the pixels come from the Core.
  3. Buffer as an option: 5 cells (0.29 Wh) hold the safe screen for 30 min with no link. That is the 'never empty' Skin of BRIEF 4.1.
  4. Bare: yes, a store of 0.1 to 1 s rides a contact bounce or a cable re-seat. It needs the cell layer, not the margin.
  5. No cell at all: the default. The Skin goes dark in a gap and re-syncs when the link returns.
