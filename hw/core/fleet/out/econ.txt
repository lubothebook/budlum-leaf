  What a failure does to the Leaf: the shadow takes over, 4 to 5 frames covered by U1 at p50 and p99 [TREE] hw/dock dockpair;
  a cold start is 133 ms at p50 and 152 ms at p99. Without any Core in reach the Leaf falls back to a far validator, 93 ms RTT: the delta class holds, video does not.
  A dock pair in one box makes the joint loss of power and primary 60 times more frequent [TREE] hw/dock: never put the shadow in the primary site.
  Recommended: R = 2 sites reach every Leaf, on two feeders; each site holds at most 50 to 60 percent of its sessions; one spare compute box per site;
  a UPS of 4 h on the fair grid, 8 h on the poor grid; the shadow of a session lives in the other site. The site cost rises by 2 times for the same Leafs.
  Blast radius: one site holds 3222 registered Leafs, up to 747 live delta sessions. One event touches all of them. [HYPOTHESIS]
  Power of the site at capacity: 4.7 kW peak, 3.3 kW average. Per registered Leaf 1.01 W; per live delta session 6.3 W against 2.19 W in the Leaf.

8 · the receipts. Part 21 has one receipt per session and second; the Core can settle them in one transaction per slot.
  one site, 3222 registered Leafs at the peak mix: 757 receipts per second (483 live, 274 idle).
  naive on chain: 7.6 MB/s with the key inline (10039 B each), 5.6 MB/s with the key by reference (7447 B).
  aggregated by the Core: one transaction per 6 s slot of 3509 B, 0.58 kB/s. The receipts stay with the Core and each Leaf holds its Merkle path.
  the ratio is 12999 times (key inline). For 1 000 000 registered Leafs at 3222 per site: 310 sites, naive 2 GB/s, aggregated 0.18 MB/s.
  the aggregate needs a rule: the Leaf attestation of each second stays checkable by a challenger for the challenge period. Gate HC-C9.
