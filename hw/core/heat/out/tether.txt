Each watt of Skin-end loss on the seat tile costs 2.95 W of sustained display power at 25 C, still air.
The same watt spread over all tiles costs 0.99 W. Where the loss sits matters by a factor of 3.0.
'sust seat' puts the path loss on the seat tile. 'sust spread' puts it in regulators spread over the tiles.
