# The allocation table of the Split, part by part

Generated by `parts.py` from `parts_data.json`. Every row cites its file. `[HYPOTHESIS]` for every derived mass.

**The rule of necessity (Ayaz, 2026-10-19).** Ayaz, 2026-10-19. A part stays in the B module only if physics forces it at the distance class. The body keeps the emitters and the tile drivers. Everything else is Core. A star marks a part on the line. The line items L1 to L12 give the reason.

**The verdict.** Body keeps the emitters, the tile drivers and the carrier of the tiles. Module is the B module. Core is the separate hardware.

**The codes after the verdict.** P must stay: physics, law or an order of Ayaz. S stays by design. M can move to a Core, with the fraction. A is added by the Split.

**The classes.** C0 is contact or tether, up to 2 m, with the radio SiPs moved whole. C0R is C0 with the radio SiPs kept. C1 is a room up to about 30 m. C2 is far.
