========================================================================
A. BANDWIDTH
========================================================================
raw 24 b/px 29.49 Gb/s, raw 30 b/px 36.86 Gb/s
  DSC 1.2b, 8 bpp (24 b in, 3:1)       9.83 Gb/s payload,  10.75 Gb/s on the wire,   76.8 Mb/s per tile
  DSC 1.2b, 10 bpp (30 b in, 3:1)     12.29 Gb/s payload,  13.43 Gb/s on the wire,   96.0 Mb/s per tile
  VDC-M 1.2, 6 bpp (30 b in, 5:1)      7.37 Gb/s payload,   8.06 Gb/s on the wire,   57.6 Mb/s per tile
design case: DSC 10 bpp, one 200 x 200 slice per tile -> 13.43 Gb/s total, 104.9 Mb/s per tile
the stream size does not change with stretch: U1 sends the warped 200 x 200 tile images.

========================================================================
B. THE 12 BAR LEAVES
========================================================================
leaf  1..12 at 0.8 mm pitch: VSYS GND D0+ D0- GND BC+ BC- GND D1- D1+ GND VSYS
mirror (front/back flip, 180 deg turn) maps the map onto itself, up to lane and polarity swap: True
two display pairs at 10 Gb/s: 19.39 Gb/s payload capacity, need 13.85 -> margin 40 %
one pair lost: VDC-M 6 bpp needs 7.82 Gb/s on 9.70 -> fits
display uses 4 leaves (D0, D1) + the shared GND; BC is the 400 Mb/s back channel
(touch at 480 Hz, hinge angles, seam sense, tile telemetry, enumeration).

========================================================================
C. THE HINGE MESH
========================================================================
128 tiles, 232 hinges (each tile joins its 4 edge neighbours at its 4 corners)
  seat top edge (3,0)       degree 3: LP optimum  4.44 Gb/s, XY 12.59, XY/YX split  7.08, LP with one root hinge broken  6.66 Gb/s
  seat bottom edge (4,15)   degree 3: LP optimum  4.44 Gb/s, XY 12.59, XY/YX split  7.08, LP with one root hinge broken  6.66 Gb/s
  seat left edge (0,7)      degree 3: LP optimum  4.44 Gb/s, XY  6.72, XY/YX split  6.24, LP with one root hinge broken  6.66 Gb/s
  seat right edge (7,8)     degree 3: LP optimum  4.44 Gb/s, XY  6.72, XY/YX split  6.24, LP with one root hinge broken  6.66 Gb/s
  verdict: plain XY routing overloads the root row; the hub runs a route table from the LP.
  the validators compute the tables (no CPU on the device); U1 loads them at enumeration.
  bound: total / degree = 4.44 Gb/s for a degree-3 seat

========================================================================
D. LANES AND LINES PER HINGE, AND THE COPPER FIT
========================================================================
  1 lane(s) x 3.2 Gb/s = 3.10 Gb/s payload: worst load 6.66 -> no (221 % used)
  2 lane(s) x 3.2 Gb/s = 6.21 Gb/s payload: worst load 6.66 -> no (111 % used)
  3 lane(s) x 3.2 Gb/s = 9.31 Gb/s payload: worst load 6.66 -> fits (74 % used)
  chosen: 3 lanes + 1 back-channel pair + 5 guard grounds = 13 lines: G D0+ D0- G D1+ D1- G D2+ D2- G BC+ BC- G
  ribbon 1.2 mm tall, 0.1 mm keep-out each end, w/s 15/15 um: 33 lines on one neutral-plane copper layer
  display share 13/33 = 39 % of the ribbon; 20 lines left for power, balance and Qi strands
  strain at 25 %: ribbon surface 0.48 %, centred 2 um Cu 0.077 % (Cu thin-film endurance ~0.2-0.3 % [GENERAL])
  strain at 41 %: ribbon surface 1.23 %, centred 2 um Cu 0.197 % (Cu thin-film endurance ~0.2-0.3 % [GENERAL])
  line R over the 0.8 mm hinge 0.46 Ohm; skin depth at 1.6 GHz 1.65 um
  hinge pair impedance ~191 Ohm diff., electrical length 1.9 deg at Nyquist, |S11| 0.023 (-32.6 dB): a lumped step, harmless

========================================================================
E. ADDRESSING AND LATENCY
========================================================================
  seat top edge (3,0)       farthest tile 19 hops
  seat bottom edge (4,15)   farthest tile 19 hops
  seat left edge (0,7)      farthest tile 15 hops
  seat right edge (7,8)     farthest tile 15 hops
  retime per hop ~25 ns -> worst 0.47 us
  rolling tile latch: one tile row of the frame arrives in 0.260 ms; emission centroid of a 4-segment PWM 0.52 ms later
  packet: [dest x 3 b | dest y 4 b | type 4 b | line 8 b | len 12 b | payload | CRC-32]
  broadcast type for frame sync; the sync carries a hop count so every hub
  latches at the same instant to within 1 us.
