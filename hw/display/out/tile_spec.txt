B. THE PWM
========================================================================
frame 4.167 ms, 12-bit PWM, LSB 1.017 us, counter clock 0.983 MHz
bit-split into 4 segments -> flicker fundamental 960 Hz
2-level PAM (low = 1/16) -> 16 bit effective range, smallest step at 1 000 nits = 15.3 mnit
uLED rise and fall ~10-50 ns [GENERAL], so the 1.02 us LSB is 20x the edge
data per uIC per frame: 144 b; per tile per frame 1.44 Mb; raw per tile 345.6 Mb/s
